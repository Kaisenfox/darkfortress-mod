DARK FORTRESS - Fabric mod for Minecraft Java 1.21.1

USE A BRAND-NEW WORLD. On first load the mod builds the fortress at world spawn
(this can take 10-60 seconds). It also sets these gamerules: spawnRadius 0,
keepInventory on, natural mob spawning off. Edit the constants at the top of
DarkFortressMod.java to change that (or set ETERNAL_DUSK = true for permanent twilight).

BUILD: push to GitHub; the included Actions workflow builds the jar
(download "darkfortress-jar" from the run), or run `gradle build` with JDK 21.
Needs Fabric Loader 0.16+ and Fabric API for 1.21.1.

WALKTHROUGH (spoilers!)
 1. You spawn at the Courtyard bonfire. Right-click it to rest (sets respawn, refills Estus).
    Dying sends you back to your last bonfire and respawns the enemies.
 2. Clear the courtyard. Ramparts (stairs on the east/west walls) have archers and 4 towers.
    The NW tower's inner wall is an Illusory Wall: right-click it for a secret chest.
 3. The ruined stairwell at the west side of the courtyard leads to the Catacombs.
    There is an illusory wall in the south side of the main corridor (west part): secret chest.
 4. The Black Knight in the big catacomb hall drops the Fortress Key. A 2nd bonfire is at the
    east end of the corridor; stairs there lead back up.
 5. Use the key on the locked gate of the Keep (north of the courtyard). 3rd bonfire inside.
 6. Stairs up (east side of the great hall) lead to the Demon of the Keep. Two phases.
    Reward: Moonlight Greatsword, Soul of a Great Demon, full Black Knight armor set.

ITEMS: Estus Flask, Soul of a Lost Undead / Great Demon (right-click = XP), Fortress Key,
Broken Straight Sword, Black Knight Greatsword, Moonlight Greatsword (right-click beam),
Lightning Spear, Pyromancy Flame, Divine Blessing, Ring of Favor, Black Knight armor x4.
Blocks: Bonfire, Locked Gate, Illusory Wall (creative tab "Dark Fortress").
